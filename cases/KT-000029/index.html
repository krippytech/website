Case KT-000029

Repeated Full-System Freezes Justified Hardware Escalation

Category: Workstation Reliability / Hardware Escalation

Status: Hardware Repaired / Post-Repair Testing Passed

The problem

A laptop repeatedly became fully unresponsive during Teams and Zoom meetings and required forced restarts. The failure returned after driver changes, operating-system repair attempts, firmware updates, peripheral isolation, and a factory re-image.

Locked lesson

A clean re-image followed by the same full-system failure is evidence that the fault domain may have moved beyond software.

Supporting principle

Temporary improvement is not durable proof. Recurrence after a known-good software baseline should change the next test.

Investigation path

Symptom scope → Cross-application comparison → Network comparison → Software remediation history → Clean re-image → Recurrence → Hardware escalation → Vendor repair → Rebuild → Stress and workflow verification

What the evidence showed

  • The failure occurred in more than one meeting application.
  • The reported symptom was a full-system freeze, not only an application crash.
  • Network performance from other devices remained normal during reported incidents.
  • The issue persisted through operating-system and driver remediation.
  • A factory re-image produced only temporary improvement.
  • The failure was witnessed during a live meeting.
  • The endpoint was escalated to the hardware vendor.
  • The returned device appeared to have received a system-board replacement and was factory-reset.
  • The endpoint was rebuilt, patched, and tested under GPU load and video-call use.
  • Post-repair testing completed without observed visual artifacts or instability.

Evidence boundary

The record supports successful post-repair testing, but it does not contain a long-duration recurrence study. This case does not claim that every meeting freeze is a hardware failure, that a re-image always rules out software, or that the exact failed hardware component was independently proven outside the vendor repair outcome.

Engineering judgment

The turning point was not simply that earlier fixes failed. It was that the same full-system behavior returned across applications after a clean software baseline. At that point, repeating broad software remediation would have added little information. Hardware escalation became the more evidence-based next step.

Verification

  • Reproduce across more than one meeting platform when practical.
  • Confirm whether the operating system freezes or only one application.
  • Record what remediation has already been attempted and whether improvement was temporary.
  • Collect vendor diagnostics and event evidence before requesting hardware repair.
  • After repair, patch the device and validate required business applications.
  • Run a controlled stress test and a real meeting test.

Technical Case · KT-000029

Repeated Full-System Freezes Justified Hardware Escalation

The endpoint froze across more than one meeting application, and the same full-system failure returned after a clean re-image.Recurrence after the known-good software baseline changed the next test from more software work to hardware escalation.

Documented by Michael Miller · Published September 9, 2026

Case ID

KT-000029

Category

Workstation Reliability / Hardware Escalation

Status

Hardware Repaired / Post-Repair Testing Passed

Technologies

Windows / Meeting Applications / GPU Stress Testing

Problem

What kind of failure was occurring?

The failure occurred in more than one meeting application. The symptom was full-system freezing, not only a single application crash, and network performance from other devices remained normal during reported incidents.

Investigation

Why did the next test move beyond software?

A clean re-image followed by the same full-system failure is evidence that the fault domain may have moved beyond software.

Temporary improvement is not durable proof. Recurrence after a known-good software baseline should change the next test.

Investigation path: Symptom scope → Cross-application comparison → Network comparison → Software remediation history → Clean re-image → Recurrence → Hardware escalation → Vendor repair → Rebuild → Stress and workflow verification

Step 1

Compare applications, system behavior, and network health

Cross-application recurrence and full-system freezing separated the symptom from one meeting client. Other devices retained normal network performance during reported incidents.

Step 2

Preserve the remediation history

The issue persisted through operating-system and driver remediation. A factory re-image produced only temporary improvement.

Step 3

Witness recurrence and escalate hardware

The failure was witnessed during a live meeting after the clean software baseline, and the endpoint was escalated to the hardware vendor.

Step 4

Rebuild and verify after vendor repair

The returned device appeared to have received a system-board replacement and was factory-reset. The endpoint was rebuilt, patched, and equipped with required business applications.

Verification

What passed after repair?

GPU load testing completed without observed visual artifacts or instability. Video-call use was included in post-repair verification.

Finding

What remains qualified?

This case does not claim every Teams or Zoom freeze is hardware, or that a clean re-image universally rules out software.

The source says the device appeared to have received a system-board replacement; the exact failed component was not independently proven beyond the vendor repair outcome.

Post-repair testing passed, but this case does not claim permanent resolution or a long-duration recurrence-free period.

This page is the proof layer for evidence-driven hardware escalation, not a replacement for the linked symptom-boundary, comparison, workstation-decision, or verification methodologies.

Experienced technical help without the traditional MSP model.

KrippyTech helps small businesses solve technology problems, improve existing systems, and make better IT decisions without requiring a long-term managed-services relationship. Learn what makes sense to learn. Hand off what should not be guessed at. Get experienced help when the work becomes complicated.

I don’t need you to depend on me for every password reset. I’d rather help you understand the easy things and be there when the work becomes complicated, risky, or time-consuming.

That is the consulting model: practical help when you need it, clear explanations when you want them, and no requirement to outsource every routine technology task.

Teach it. Handle it. Partner on it.

Different businesses need different levels of involvement. The engagement should fit the problem, not force the problem into a support package.

Teach it

Learn the routine work safely

If a task makes sense to handle internally, I can explain the process, document it clearly, and show you where the safe boundaries are.

Handle it

Hand off the technical work

If you do not want to spend your time learning or troubleshooting it, bring me in to work the problem, complete the project, or manage the change.

What KrippyTech helps with

The focus is project work, troubleshooting, modernization, security improvement, planning, simplification, and technical second looks.

Solve

Difficult technical problems

Microsoft 365 issues, identity and access problems, infrastructure failures, networking, file access, remote access, recurring workstation problems, and technical situations that have become difficult to untangle.

Improve

Make the environment easier to operate

Modernize aging technology, simplify existing systems, improve backups and security, reduce fragile processes, clean up permissions, and remove unnecessary complexity.

Plan

Make better technology decisions

Technology roadmaps, Microsoft 365 and cloud planning, migrations, equipment replacement, infrastructure changes, security priorities, and independent review before committing money to a vendor or solution.

Review

Get a second set of eyes

Review an existing recommendation, recurring problem, planned project, technology spend, licensing decision, or environment that does not feel as reliable or understandable as it should.

Simplify

Reduce unnecessary cost and complexity

Look for duplicated services, unused licensing, avoidable recurring costs, aging systems, or technology that is more complicated than the business actually needs.

Document

Leave useful knowledge behind

Capture the environment, recurring procedures, risks, ownership, and next steps so the business is easier to support after the engagement than it was before.

Technology should support the business

Before recommending another subscription, server, security product, or cloud service, start with the business problem.

Keep

What is already working?

Not every older system needs to be replaced. Good technology decisions begin by understanding what is already reliable and useful.

Fix

What is creating risk?

Identify fragile systems, weak security, poor recovery options, unclear ownership, recurring failures, and technical debt that deserves attention.

Remove

What no longer earns its cost?

Review unnecessary subscriptions, duplicated products, unused licenses, outdated services, and complexity that adds cost without adding useful capability.

Plan

What deserves investment next?

Build priorities around business impact, risk, budget, and growth instead of a generic list of products somebody wants to sell.

What this is not

KrippyTech consulting is intentionally different from a traditional managed-services relationship.

Not dependency

You do not need to outsource every task

If your staff can safely handle something internally, there is no reason to make it dependent on an outside provider.

Not a product quota

The recommendation should fit the need

Not every problem needs another subscription, appliance, or enterprise platform. The technology should earn its place.

Not an unlimited help desk

Defined consulting instead of MSP packaging

The focus is technical consulting, projects, troubleshooting, planning, and hands-on work where the engagement and outcome are understood.

How an initial engagement begins

There is no published package, price, schedule, or service-level promise. The first step is a concise email with enough context to decide whether a conversation makes sense. KrippyTech does not currently offer emergency, 24/7, help-desk, or managed-services coverage.

01

Send the context

Include the problem or planned change, the relevant technologies, what has been observed, and what outcome you are trying to reach.

02

Confirm fit and scope

Before work begins, fit, scope, availability, access, responsibilities, and commercial terms must be agreed.

03

Choose the right next step

An initial conversation may lead to guidance, a defined project discussion, or the conclusion that KrippyTech is not the right fit.

Good technical work starts by defining the problem clearly.

Practical learning for real IT work

MSP University

MSP University is KrippyTech's learning home for technicians, solo IT administrators, and MSP professionals who need a repeatable way to investigate, understand, and solve Microsoft-focused IT problems. Learn the system. Follow the evidence. Verify the result.

Explore the learning path Browse Tutorials

Learn in the order real troubleshooting happens

MSP University is organized around the thinking technicians use when a ticket arrives: understand the environment, gather evidence, isolate the problem, make the right change, and prove the outcome.

01 · Understand

Know what you are looking at

Build the foundation across Microsoft 365, identity, Windows, Active Directory, networking, cloud, and hybrid infrastructure.

02 · Investigate

Collect useful evidence

Learn which portal views, PowerShell checks, logs, permissions, traces, and health signals answer the question instead of creating more noise.

03 · Resolve

Make deliberate changes

Separate discovery from remediation, understand the blast radius, verify authorization and scope, and make the smallest change that addresses the actual cause.

04 · Verify

Prove the fix worked

Re-test the original failure, confirm expected behavior, document what changed, and leave enough evidence for the next technician to understand the case.
